FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where does my dog's data go?

It stays on your phone. Olme has no accounts or servers and does not connect to the internet: your data is not sent to anyone, not even to the people who made the app.

Is Olme free?

Yes, Olme is free, with no advertising and no purchases.

Can I back up my data?

Yes. With “Export backup” you create a file with all the data, photos and documents, to save wherever you like. The file is not password-protected: keep it somewhere you trust.

Does Olme replace the vet or the official record?

No. Olme gives no veterinary advice and does not replace the passport, certificates or your dog's registration in the dog registry.
