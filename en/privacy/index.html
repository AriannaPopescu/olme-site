Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27

In short

Olme does not collect or send any data. There are no accounts and no servers: everything you write stays on your phone.

Contact

For questions about this policy write to olmepet@gmail.com.

If you write to us

E-mails you send to olmepet@gmail.com are received and handled by whoever publishes the app, shown as the developer on the App Store or Google Play listing.

The controller of this processing is Arianna Popescu, who publishes Olme as a private individual.

We use your address and what you write only to reply to you. E-mails go through Google's Gmail service and are deleted within 12 months of the last reply.

You can ask at any time to see or delete your e-mails by writing to the same address. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

What data the app handles

  • The details of your dogs that you enter: name, breed, birth, microchip, health record, weight, agenda, behaviour, activity, training and food.
  • The dog photos and the documents you add.
  • Free notes, which may also contain details of other people, such as the vet's name and phone number.
  • Your settings: language, appearance and the unit of activity durations (minutes or hours).

The app does not ask for data about your health and you do not need to enter data about yourself.

Where the data stays

The data is kept in the app's private folder on your phone. Photos are saved again without location and other camera data. PDF files are copied as they are, including the data inside the file, such as its author or the program that created it.

Network, tracking and advertising

Olme does not connect to the internet, uses no analytics or crash reporting tools, shows no advertising and builds no profiles. Links you tap, such as the source of the reference weight, open in the phone's browser, outside the app.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications, scheduled on the phone. No data leaves the phone. The notification title is the dog's name and the text is the reminder title: they also appear on the lock screen, if the phone allows it.

Phone backups

  • On iPhone, Olme's data is part of the phone's iCloud backup, if it is turned on.
  • On Android, Google's automatic backup saves the database, but not photos and documents.

These copies follow Apple's or Google's rules.

Files you export

With "Export backup" you create a file with all the data, photos and documents. The file is not encrypted. You save or share it wherever you choose, for example in Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive: from then on the copy follows the rules of the service you chose. Olme does not send the file anywhere.

The same applies to PDF files you open from Olme: the phone asks which app to open them with, and the app you choose receives a copy of the file.

How to delete the data

  • In Settings, "Delete all data" deletes the data, photos, documents and scheduled notifications.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes the data on the phone. Copies in system backups and files you exported remain until you delete them.

This web page

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no resources from other sites: fonts and images are all hosted here.

The site is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages). When you visit a page, GitHub logs and stores your IP address for the security of the service, even if you have no GitHub account. GitHub's rules apply to this processing, as described in its privacy policy (GitHub General Privacy Statement).
