Terms and notices

Last updated: 2026-10-27

What Olme is

Olme is a digital record book to keep your dogs' details in order on your phone. It is free and needs no account.

It does not replace the vet

Olme gives no veterinary advice, diagnoses, doses or rations. For your dog's health the vet's opinion always comes first.

It is not an official document

Olme is not an official document and does not replace the dog's passport, veterinary certificates or the dog's registration in the dog registry required by law.

Reference weight

The reference weight range is general, indicative information, based on breed standards or on size. The source is shown in the app. It is not an assessment of your dog's health.

Your data and backups

You enter the data and you are responsible for checking it is correct. The data stays only on the phone: if the phone breaks, is lost or the app is uninstalled, the data may be lost. That is why it is worth exporting a backup from time to time and keeping it somewhere other than the phone.

Warranties

Olme is free and offered as it is. Within the limits allowed by law, we do not guarantee that the app is always available or free of errors, or that the data will be preserved. The rights the law grants you that cannot be excluded always remain unaffected.
